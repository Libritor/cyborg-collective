Live Gaussian splatting

looking for a headset that is scanning…

what this is

When someone scans with a Meta Quest 3S and switches the live view on, every reading of the headset's depth sensing arrives here within a second and becomes one Gaussian: a soft disc lying on the surface it hit, coloured from the camera. They are drawn as Gaussian splats as they come, one for each 2 cm of surface, and a surface seen from behind is left out so that a room can be looked into.

These Gaussians are placed, not trained. After the scan a laptop trains a splat from the pictures, which takes a few minutes, and that one appears in the list of scans. The relay passes the live ones on and keeps none.
