The Chains of Margoth

One bar per 1.8 s from the real generator (seed 5, A minor). C climbs 0.2 → 0.95 over 20 bars, holds for 20, then drops to 0.25 for 4: a drop after a peak is what arms the open chord. Where the drop lands on a held chord the ring shows armed and the open chord follows on the next change (bar 84 → 85 in the second cycle); where it lands on a change bar the generator consumes the flag in that same bar, so that release arrives with no preceding armed bar (the first cycle's bar 40). No audio: the level shown in the bar is derived from the bar's own content, not from a signal.
